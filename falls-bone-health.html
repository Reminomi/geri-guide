Falls & Bone Health Toolkit

Multifactorial falls-risk assessment and secondary fracture-prevention pathway.

Important: These checklists structure a multifactorial assessment; they do not replace it. Any older person presenting after a fall, or with ≥2 falls in 12 months, an injurious fall, or a gait/balance concern warrants full multifactorial assessment regardless of individual module scores below.

Practical pathway

StepDetail
1 · ScreenAsk about falls in the past 12 months at every review; a single fall with injury, or ≥2 falls, or a gait/balance complaint triggers full assessment.
2 · Multifactorial assessmentHistory, gait/balance/strength exam, postural BP, vision, cognition, footwear, medication review, home hazard review.
3 · Targeted interventionIndividualised — not a generic exercise handout. Strength/balance program (physiotherapy), medication deprescribing, vitamin D if deficient, vision/hearing correction, home modification (OT), cardiac work-up if syncope/arrhythmia suspected.
4 · Bone healthAny fragility fracture (fall from standing height or less) triggers osteoporosis work-up regardless of DEXA timing — treat the fracture as diagnostic of high risk pending formal assessment.
5 · ReviewReassess falls frequency/severity and functional trajectory at follow-up; falls risk is dynamic, not a one-off label.

Reference: fracture risk factors independent of BMD

CategoryFactors
Non-modifiableAge, female sex, prior fragility fracture, parental hip fracture history, early menopause, previous long-term corticosteroid use, rheumatoid arthritis, other secondary osteoporosis causes
ModifiableLow BMI, smoking, alcohol excess, low calcium/vitamin D intake, physical inactivity, falls risk itself
Secondary causes to excludeHyperthyroidism, hyperparathyroidism, hypogonadism, malabsorption/coeliac disease, chronic kidney disease, myeloma, long-term corticosteroids, other bone-active medications

Guide only — not a protocol. Confirm current local falls-prevention and osteoporosis-treatment guidelines before clinical use. No patient information entered here is stored or transmitted.
