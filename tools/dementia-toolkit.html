Dementia Subtype Interactive Toolkit

Structured clinical decision support for common neurocognitive syndromes and dementia subtypes.

Important: These checklists do not diagnose disease or replace published criteria, collateral history, examination, neuropsychology, laboratory testing, imaging, biomarkers or specialist review. Acute or rapidly progressive change requires urgent medical assessment.

Core references

  • Alzheimer’s Association DETeCD-ADRD pathway
  • Gorno-Tempini PPA criteria
  • VASCOG-2 vascular cognitive-disorder criteria
  • DIAMOND-Lewy resources

Use current local guidelines and the original publications before making or documenting a diagnosis. No patient information entered here is stored or transmitted.
