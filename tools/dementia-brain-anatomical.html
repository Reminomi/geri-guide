About this model & sources

Model: "Brain, Male" (NIH 3D, entry 3DPX-020960) — 283 individually segmented anatomical structures, built from the Allen Institute's Comprehensive Cellular-Resolution Atlas of the Adult Human Brain, mirrored across hemispheres and fitted to Visible Human Project proportions.

Source citation: Ding SL, Royall JJ, Sunkin SM, et al. Comprehensive cellular-resolution atlas of the adult human brain. J Comp Neurol. 2016;524(16):3127–3481.

Licence: CC BY 4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution). Model courtesy of NIH 3D / Human Reference Atlas program.

Clinical involvement mapping (which structures light up for which subtype) is this app's own synthesis from the same evidence base as the NCD Diagnostic Algorithm — it is not part of the original anatomical dataset.

No patient information entered or stored here. Structure names follow Allen Institute nomenclature.
